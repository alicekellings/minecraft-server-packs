SOURCE OFFER — SkyBlock Menus Lite
=================================

This package ships CONFIGURATION ONLY. It does not redistribute any plugin
binary, so no source-code offer is required under the GPL/LGPL/EPL for this
package.

For your reference, the plugins this pack is built against are fetched on your
machine by install.bat / install.sh, straight from their own upstream projects:

  BentoBox          https://github.com/BentoBoxWorld/BentoBox
  BSkyBlock         https://github.com/BentoBoxWorld/BSkyBlock
  Level             https://github.com/BentoBoxWorld/Level
  PlaceholderAPI    https://github.com/PlaceholderAPI/PlaceholderAPI
  DeluxeMenus       https://github.com/HelpChat/DeluxeMenus

The server core (Paper) is also downloaded by you:

  Paper             https://github.com/PaperMC/Paper

If you have any question about third-party components, use the support channel
listed in README.md.
